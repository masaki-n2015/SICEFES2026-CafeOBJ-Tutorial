Specification and Verification of Real-Time Systems Using the OTS/CafeOBJ Method: A Tutorial

Date and Time: Monday, September 14, 9:30-12:00
Room: 303

Organizers

Organizer: Prof. Masaki Nakamura, Toyama Prefectural University

Co-organizer: Prof. Kazutoshi Sakakibara, Toyama Prefectural University

Statement of Objective

To provide a clear introduction to the OTS/CafeOBJ method for modeling and verifying multitask real-time systems. In this approach, systems are modeled as observational transition systems (OTSs), and their specifications are written in CafeOBJ, an algebraic specification language. Participants will learn how to describe system behavior and time constraints using the CafeOBJ language/system, and how to verify safety properties using the proof score method through concrete examples.

Topics: CafeOBJ, Observational transition system, Formal methods, Real-time Hybrid system

Intended Audience

This tutorial is intended for researchers, engineers, and students who are interested in formal methods for concurrent and real-time systems. No prior experience with CafeOBJ is required, although basic knowledge of mathematics, logic, and programming is helpful. In this tutorial, we first introduce the basic ideas of the OTS/CafeOBJ method, and then explain how to model and describe time constraints in OTS/CafeOBJ specifications through a small example. Then, as case studies, we present Fischer’s mutual exclusion protocol and an autonomous vehicle group control system.

Speaker

Prof. Masaki Nakamura, Toyama Prefectural University

Masaki Nakamura is a professor at Toyama Prefectural University. He received his PhD in information science from JAIST (Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) in 2002. He was an assistant professor at JAIST from 2002 to 2008 and at Kanazawa University from 2008 to 2011, an associate professor at Toyama Prefectural University from 2011 to 2024. His research interest includes software engineering, formal methods, algebraic specification and term rewriting.
Recently, he received the 2025 Tomoda Paper Award from the Society of Instrument and Control Engineers (SICE), Japan. A co-authored survey paper on formal verification using Proof Score methods for OTS/CafeOBJ specifications was published in ACM Computing Surveys.

Program

  1. Introduction (5 min)
  2. The OTS/CafeOBJ Method Overview (50 min)
  3. Modeling Multitask Real-Time Systems in OTS/CafeOBJ Specifications (40 min)
  4. Case Study: Fischer’s Protocol (20 min)
  5. Case Study: Autonomous Vehicle Control System (20 min)
  6. Conclusion (5 min)
